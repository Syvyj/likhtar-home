# Likhtar Home — site direction

**Visual thesis.** A dark room with one warm light. The page is graphite; whatever matters is lit by
the lantern's amber, the way the launcher lights the way to tonight's film.

**Hero focal asset.** The owner's tent-by-the-lake scene with the lantern, full bleed. A pool of
light around the lantern breathes slowly and leans toward the pointer.

**Type.** Playfair Display (500–700, italic for accents) for headings — cinematic, warm, Cyrillic.
Manrope (the launcher's own face) for everything else. Both self-hosted, SIL OFL 1.1.

**Colour.** `--night #13110E` · `--graphite #1F1B17` · `--card #27231E` · `--amber #E8C27A` ·
`--glow #FFD27A` · `--paper #FFFAF2` · `--muted #BFB6A8`.

**Sections.** Hero → before/after (pinned, scrubbed reveal) → the tour on a TV you can drive with
the arrow keys ("remote mode") → bring your own AI → safe to try, easy to undo → privacy → how it
grew → support → final call → footer.

**Motion.** GSAP + ScrollTrigger. Headings rise word by word. The before/after wipe is the only
scrubbed, pinned sequence. Everything else is a one-shot reveal. Reduced motion: final states at once.

**Smooth scroll.** Lenis (Locomotive Scroll is heavier and owns layout; Lenis only smooths native
scroll and plugs into ScrollTrigger cleanly). Off under reduced motion.

**Three.js.** No. The only "effect" is a pool of light, which a CSS radial gradient does at no cost.

**Sound.** Web Audio, synthesised (no files): a soft wooden tick when the TV changes channel in the
tour, a warm two-note chime when the before/after finishes and when the lantern is clicked. Only on
the visitor's own actions, never on load; one toggle in the header, remembered per browser.

**Assets.** Lantern renders and the tent scene — generated by the owner. Screenshots — our own, from
the public build on the Google TV emulator and the owner's box (stock Google TV avatar masked).
Fonts — Google Fonts files, self-hosted. GSAP (standard license), Lenis (MIT) — `vendor/`.
