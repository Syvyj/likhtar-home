Bring your own AI · optional

Ask for a film in your own words.

An AI film advisor that works in any country, needs no Google account and runs on the model you choose — in your language, on any TV or box with Android TV.

  • Your key, your model. Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, OpenRouter — or a model on your own computer with Ollama or LM Studio, free and private.
  • Straight to the provider. Your request goes directly to the AI provider you choose. Likhtar's server doesn't receive it.
  • We check every title. Each suggestion is matched against TMDB by title and year before it's shown.
  • Season recaps. A short retelling of the last season, from the episode summaries.

Easy to install, easy to make yours

Your phone does the hard part.

  1. 01

    Install from your phone

    Turn on debugging on the TV, open Likhtar Installer on an Android phone, press Find the TV, then Install. No computer, no USB stick.

  2. 02

    Arrange it from the sofa

    Scan the QR code in settings: put the sections in your order, choose which Cinema rows show, hide the services you don't use.

  3. 03

    Never type with the remote

    Passwords, playlist addresses, links and files go from your phone straight to the TV.

Safe to try, easy to undo

Try it without losing the screen you know.

  1. 01

    Install and look around

    Open it like any app. Nothing on the TV changes until you ask.

  2. 02

    Make it your home screen

    One confirmation on the TV. The stock launcher is disabled, not removed — and your Wi‑Fi is checked first.

  3. 03

    Changed your mind?

    Settings → Remote and startup → Bring back the stock home screen. And if Likhtar ever crashes three times in five minutes, it hands the home screen back by itself. Uninstalling? Press that button first — otherwise the TV is left without a home screen (how to fix a black screen).

Your data stays yours.

Stays on your TVHistory, lists, profiles, PINs and settings.

Stored encryptedKeys, tokens, passwords and playlist addresses — with a key that never leaves the device.

No ads, no analyticsNo account, no sign-in, nothing sold. Film data comes through a small read-only gateway that keeps no request log.

How it grew

From a plugin to a home screen.

  1. Jan 2026

    A small plugin that gathered studio collections into a media app's sidebar.

  2. Mar 2026

    Likhtar appears — a custom home page with banners and title logos, Ukrainian first.

  3. Aug 5

    Likhtaryk, the film advisor, is born — on a local model at home.

  4. Aug 11

    The plugin hits the walls of someone else's app. The native launcher begins.

  5. Sep

    Reality, title pages, franchises, a focus system rebuilt for the remote.

  6. Oct

    A public build without a home server, settings from your phone, bring your own AI.

Where it began is still public: the original plugin on GitHub. The launcher's repository starts fresh with its first public release.
